Expression OS Method

Speak English without a script

You understand English — then freeze when it’s your turn. Expression OS is a simple AI practice system: give the model a real transcript as the answer key, then retell out loud until you can say it clearly. Not another chat that only says “great job.”

Who this is for

  • Adults who get it but can’t say it
  • People tired of AI chat with no standard and nothing left after the session
  • Anyone who wants clear self-expression — persuasion and trust — not tourist phrases
